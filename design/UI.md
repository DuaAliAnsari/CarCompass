# CarCompass interface

The site follows a product landing layout, not a dashboard.

## Look

- Full white page, black type, gray muted text.
- Top nav: page links on the left, CARCOMPASS centered, status and CTA on the right.
- Hero: large headline on the left, car art on the right, black pill CTA with an arrow.
- Specs strip under the hero: served MAE, range coverage, listing count.
- Second band: experiments story, a tall visual panel, and small fact cards.
- Estimate sits lower on the home page as section 03.
